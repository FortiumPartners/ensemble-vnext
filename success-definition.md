**Coverage of the must-pass objectives.** O1 (no close with an unfiled waived row; file, label,
record, refuse) is proven by FS-1–FS-4 and FS-7–FS-13. O2 (no tracker means no waiver, and the
report says why) by FS-15 and FS-17–FS-20. O3 (filed at most once across `file-followups`, a
resumed run and a fix round) by FS-22 and FS-23. Every objective has at least one criterion.

**Considered and not written as criteria.** The source's statement that `close-issue` "already
closes only the one issue a work's PRD/TRD header or branch cites" describes existing behaviour,
not a change, so it is not a criterion here beyond the added sentence in FS-24. The remark that,
for a feature, "the close gate has already filed every waived row by the time `/ship-build`
runs" is stated as a consequence ("a guard, not a second path"), and is covered by FS-22's
`file-followups` leg rather than a row of its own.
