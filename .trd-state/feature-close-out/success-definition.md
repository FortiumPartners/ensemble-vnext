## Objectives that did not become a criterion

- **`--accept` on a feature judged `done` or `done-with-gaps`.** The owner decision says the
  override is "used only when the judgement is `not-done`" (line 118), but not what happens if it
  is passed anyway — rejected, ignored, or recorded. No criterion checks that case; the behaviour
  is unspecified.
- **Whether a judgement is correct in general.** Only fixtures with an unambiguous right answer
  (FS-11, FS-13, FS-14, taken from the source's own example at line 114–115) are checkable. The
  quality of the model's judgement on a genuinely borderline feature has no checkable form.
- **OQ-2** (line 103, audit verdict recorded, not gated) is folded into FS-9 and FS-12 rather than
  given its own row; the owner decisions made the verdict one input to the judgement.
- **Grounding F's test list** (line 93–96) and the router's FLOW hint listing commands (line 91) are
  observations about the codebase, not objectives, and yield no criterion.
- **The three not-absorbed items** (line 63–65) are out of scope by the source's own statement;
  only the docs-as-built note (FS-34) and the honest closing of testing-phase (FS-27) come from them.
