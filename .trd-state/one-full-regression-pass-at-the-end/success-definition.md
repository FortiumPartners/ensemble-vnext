`## Intended Change` or `## Decision` section; where a line of the source's own Objectives

re-walking it. "Covered files" are the repo-relative paths the `criterionCovers` arg maps to a

the `filesChanged` of a Debug pass at an iteration greater than or equal to that `provenAt`.

## Considered and not written as separate criteria

- **Both commands passing `criterionCovers`, and the new `live-evidence.js derive-criteria`
  CLI** (lines 67-69, 99-103). These are the plumbing that FS-2, FS-4 and FS-6 depend on; a
  wrong or missing covers map shows up as those criteria failing. Not split out, to keep the
  definition about outcomes.
- **Covers matching agrees with custody's `coverMatcher` (directory prefix, glob)** (lines
  101-103). The same reasoning: a mismatch surfaces as FS-2 or FS-6 failing on a glob or
  directory cover. The parity test the source names is the plan's, not an outcome.
- **A criterion resumed as met is scoped against this invocation's Debug changes only**
  (lines 109-110). The source records this as a known limit (Could Not Verify), not a behaviour
  to prove; writing it as a criterion would certify a gap.
- **4.17.5's re-proof retry and scoped re-proof at ship read the regression pass's verdicts**
  (line 33). The source gives no line beyond the objective itself describing how those read
  state; the reading custody does is covered by FS-12.
