## Summary

Six framework changes that make Ensemble's `/ship-build` cheaper: the custody anchor is taken after `/audit-build`'s own fixes so a ship does not start by re-proving; custody re-proves only the criteria a change can affect (covers recorded for every criterion, docs, dependency and CI-config change classes, `OWNER-CALLS.md` as a record file, tier rules as a per-product setting); a re-proof gets one retry of its unmet criteria; a CI fix is sized to the failure; the merge step loops on a red merge-up head instead of ending STUCK; and re-proof results are written to `shipped.json` for Forge's ship record.
